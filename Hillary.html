Common questions

Can I book more than one service at once?

Yes. Many clients need a website and branding together, or IT support alongside document preparation. Add as many services as you like and describe how they connect.

My task is small. Is it still worth booking?

Absolutely. A single CV, a spreadsheet fix or a slow computer is a perfectly good reason to get in touch. Small problems are often the ones that cost you the most time.

What happens after I send my enquiry?

I review what you need and reply with a suitable solution, a timeline and a quotation, so you can decide with full information.

I am not sure which service I need. What should I do?

Describe the problem in your own words in the project details. You do not need technical language. Together we can work out the right approach.

Do you accept referrals?

Gladly. If you do not need these services yourself, a referral to someone who might is a big compliment and always appreciated.
